# MarketingTeam: brand and claim context

*State: starter context. It deliberately contains no invented product facts.*

## Scope

Create advertising and campaign materials for goods aimed at Hearthstone and Warcraft audiences. The team may work on physical merchandise, digital products, services, or editorial offers only after the specific offer is documented below.

## Non-negotiables

- Hearthstone and Warcraft references identify the audience or subject; they do **not** establish an official relationship with Blizzard Entertainment.
- A price, discount, stock level, delivery promise, compatibility claim, game claim, customer result, review, or “official” label is publishable only with a linked source or user-supplied evidence.
- Product images and game/IP assets require a confirmed right to use. If the source is unknown, propose a text-first or original-visual concept instead.
- Do not market prohibited, deceptive, or policy-sensitive offers without first identifying the product, destination market, fulfilment method, and applicable ad-platform policy.

## Voice

Enthusiastic and knowledgeable, but never falsely insider or overly promising. Use game terminology only when it helps a verified product benefit. Prefer a specific benefit, proof, and clear CTA over “легендарный”, “лучший”, or meta claims without evidence.
