# MarketingTeam rules

This workspace prepares advertising materials for goods connected with Hearthstone and Warcraft. Before working on an offer, read `brand-context.md` and, if present, `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

## Facts and rights

- Treat a product card, price, availability, delivery terms, permitted territories, official-status claims, and discount deadlines as unconfirmed until they have a source supplied by the user or a current cited source.
- Do not imply Blizzard Entertainment endorsement, partnership, licensing, or an official product status without evidence. Do not use protected logos, game art, screenshots, or character imagery unless the user confirms the right to use the particular asset.
- Verify time-sensitive game claims (patch, mode, expansion, card availability, release timing) before including them. Do not invent meta rankings, player results, reviews, or scarcity.
- If the item, its fulfilment, or platform-policy fit is unclear, name the missing fact as `[НУЖНО ПОДТВЕРДИТЬ: ...]`; do not conceal it with stronger copy.

## Operating boundary

- Create strategy, creatives, landing-page text, tracking plans, and drafts here. Do not create or publish ads, spend money, contact customers, or connect an advertising account unless the user expressly requests it and provides the required access.
- Prefer Russian copy unless the task asks for another market or language. Adapt each ad to the actual product, segment, platform, and landing page rather than reusing generic gaming claims.

## Visual advertising

- Generate an original product-focused scene, not an imitation of Blizzard key art. Treat a supplied logo, screenshot, card art, character art, or product photo as an input with a documented role and confirmed usage right.
- Do not rely on generated text for a price, code, legal disclosure, or the primary CTA. Reserve readable negative space and add approved final text in the delivery workflow; visually inspect any text that must remain in the generated image.
- Keep the item recognisable. If a real product image is supplied, preserve its shape, material, branding, and important details rather than replacing it with a look-alike.

## Skill routing

Use `$warcraft-product-advertising` to turn a factual product brief into an advertising package. It routes to the installed paid-ads, ad-creative, copywriting, social, video, analytics, competitor, launch, customer-research, email, CRO, product-marketing, GPT Image creative, and visual-review skills when their narrower work is needed.
