# General Domain Adapter

Load this adapter for cross-domain or non-specialized topics. It adds scope controls without changing the shared research method.

## Scope axes

Identify the axes that can change the answer:

- jurisdiction and geography;
- population and inclusion criteria;
- date and event/data window;
- operational definition of the outcome;
- stakeholder perspective;
- observational versus causal question;
- historical, current, or forecast intent.

## Source routing

- Laws, policies, and official counts: responsible institution and primary text first.
- Scientific claims: original peer-reviewed study, protocol, dataset, or systematic review as appropriate; check retractions/corrections and study design.
- Market or company facts: filings, official reports, regulator data, and audited records before media summaries.
- Public statements: original transcript, recording, post, or publication.
- Recommendations: combine outcome evidence with context, costs, failure modes, and stakeholder constraints.

## Frequent failure modes

- using a global claim for one geography or demographic;
- confusing a publication date with the data window;
- treating correlation as causation;
- substituting a press release for the underlying study;
- quoting a headline that overstates the source;
- comparing values with different definitions or denominators;
- giving false precision where measurement is uncertain.

For medical, legal, or financial decisions, apply the stricter current-authority and uncertainty requirements of the environment and avoid replacing professional advice.
