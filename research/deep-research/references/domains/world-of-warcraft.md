# World of Warcraft Domain Adapter

Load with the gaming adapter for World of Warcraft research.

## Required context

Record:

- game branch: Retail, Classic era, progression variant, PTR/Beta, or other;
- region and live/PTR status;
- expansion, season, patch, build, and hotfix date;
- activity: raid, Mythic+, PvP, delves, open world, professions, collection, or lore;
- class, specialization, role, encounter/dungeon, difficulty, key range, and rating bracket when relevant.

Never transfer mechanics or tuning across Retail, Classic, PTR, and live without proof.

## Source routing

- Rules, abilities, releases: Blizzard patch notes, hotfixes, official APIs/data, and in-game text.
- Hidden or edge mechanics: reliable datamining plus reproducible logs/tests; distinguish live, PTR, and inferred behavior.
- Performance: current combat logs or datasets with encounter, difficulty, percentile, sample, kill/wipe, and filter context.
- Strategy: encounter evidence plus qualified class/role experts and current community testing.
- Lore: primary in-game/source texts first, then official publications and clearly labeled interpretation.

## Statistical cautions

Tier lists are summaries, not foundational Warcraft data. Check spec representation, encounter composition, key level, percentile, gear, augmentation/support effects, survival, utility, and selection bias. A high aggregate parse does not prove universal value.

## Freshness cautions

Hotfixes can alter tuning without a major patch. Verify the effective date and whether a data window spans the change. Mark PTR/Beta findings separately from live evidence.

## Community cautions

Separate theorycraft, simulation, logs, guide repetition, and anecdotal play. Multiple guides copying one class Discord conclusion share a lineage unless they independently test it.
