# Gaming Domain Adapter

Load for live-service games, competitive strategy, mechanics, meta, builds, guides, and community analysis.

## Required context

Record game, mode, platform/region if relevant, patch, season, expansion or ruleset, rank/MMR bracket, and research date. Do not combine PvE/PvP, ranked/casual, formats, modes, or brackets without justification.

## Claim routing

- Mechanics/rules: official text, developer statements, patch notes, game files/datamining, reproducible tests.
- Edge interactions: official rule plus reproducible test or video evidence; distinguish intended behavior from observed bug.
- Meta strength: large current samples with metric definitions and rank/mode filters.
- Strategy: current statistics plus elite-player reasoning; add broader community patterns for usability and exceptions.
- History: archived patch notes and versioned sources.

## Expert signals

Distinguish casual user, high-rank player, tournament player, current-mode specialist, content creator, developer, theorycrafter/tester, and statistical analyst. Verify claims of rank or role where feasible. Follower count and confidence of delivery are not enough.

## Statistical cautions

- Pick rate can bias win-rate interpretation.
- Top-player data may not generalize to lower brackets.
- Average placement, top-four rate, win rate, matchup rate, and play rate answer different questions.
- Early-patch data can be unstable and selected by fast adopters.
- Composition/build stats can suffer survivorship bias because failed attempts never reach the recorded end state.
- Balance changes and hotfixes can split an apparently single data window.

## Community cautions

Segment discussions by patch, mode, rank, and whether posters are reacting to the same creator or clip. Treat clips and screenshots as examples until provenance and context are verified.

## Completion additions

A gaming branch is not complete until version compatibility is explicit and common counterexamples or conditional exceptions have been sought.
