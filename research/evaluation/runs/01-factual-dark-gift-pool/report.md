## Answer

“Dark Gift pool” is ambiguous and must be split into two questions.

1. **What can be offered?** This is documented. The minion side is controlled by the turn-based Tavern Tier schedule, mechanic-specific and individual-minion exclusions, and—starting on turn 6—a guarantee that one offer matches the player's most common minion type. The gift side is controlled by each gift's min/max turn window, compatibility with the offered minion, lobby and prior-use conditions, plus a special subset for Tras'tath. The original developer explainer is only a baseline: patch 36.2.2 changed several windows, values, exclusions, and the Tras'tath subset.
2. **Does taking the minion remove a finite copy from the normal Tavern pool?** The inspected official announcement, developer explainer, and current patch notes do not explicitly say. A current Reddit thread says yes, but provides no authoritative citation. Treat this as unverified community knowledge, not a confirmed Blizzard rule.

Patch 36.4 was checked and contains no later Dark Gift rules change, so the 36.2.2 overlay remains the current inspected rules layer.

## Confidence

- Offer eligibility: **HIGH**.
- Patch precedence and current overlay: **HIGH**.
- Shared-copy depletion: **LOW / unresolved**.
