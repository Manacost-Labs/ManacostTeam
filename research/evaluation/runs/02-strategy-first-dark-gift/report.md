## Answer

There is no evidence-backed universal turn for the first Dark Gift. Use a state-based rule:

- **Press now** when spending 3 Gold on the current shop is weak, you need immediate tempo, or the Gift cleanly repairs an awkward curve.
- **Usually preserve the option through turns 4-6** when your board is stable. Later turns improve the official Tavern Tier bands, and turn 6 guarantees one offer from your most common minion type.
- **Do not delay mechanically** if doing so means buying low-value units or taking avoidable damage. The future-offer benefit must exceed the current tempo loss.

Across four current community discussions, the most repeatable pattern is “one early tempo use, later charges kept flexible,” commonly around turns 4-6. This is a conditional community heuristic, not a statistically measured optimum. Targeted searches did not find public HSGuru or HSReplay data for first-use timing.

Patch 36.2.2 changed many Dark Gift values and timing windows; exact advice from before 2026-08-18 should be rechecked.
