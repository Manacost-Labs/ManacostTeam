The current Dark Gift pool is determined in two stages. Minion offers use turn-based Tavern Tier bands, mechanic and named-minion exclusions, and from turn 6 a guarantee that one offer matches the player's most common minion type. The gift paired with an offered minion must satisfy its turn window, text compatibility, lobby and prior-use conditions, and any special Tras'tath pool rule.

The launch explainer must be overlaid with patch 36.2.2. Patch 36.4 was checked and contains no later Dark Gift rules change. This answers offer eligibility; it does not prove whether the selected minion consumes a finite Tavern copy.
