# Research Report

Research ID: `RES-20260828T073255Z-CE9BFDDA`

In Poland, daylight-saving time begins on 29 March 2026: clocks move from 02:00 CET to 03:00 CEST. It ends on 25 October 2026: clocks move from 03:00 CEST back to 02:00 CET. Both transitions correspond to 01:00 UTC.

The controlling national basis is the Polish Prime Minister's regulation of 4 March 2022 for 2022-2026. An EU schedule was discovered but its page was JavaScript-blocked, so it was not used as claim evidence.
