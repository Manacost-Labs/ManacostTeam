Research ID: `RES-20260828T073255Z-DCC7F308`

There is no defensible metric-free answer. By announced site power, the Portsmouth, Ohio project is a leading candidate at an expected 10 GW and is described by DOE as the planned world's largest AI data center. But the independent report says it was still entering construction in 2026, so it cannot be presented as the largest operational facility as of 2026-08-28.

A ranking must first choose operational versus announced status and a metric such as site power, IT power, floor area, accelerator count, or delivered compute. Without that choice and a comparable global inventory, the correct answer is conditional.
