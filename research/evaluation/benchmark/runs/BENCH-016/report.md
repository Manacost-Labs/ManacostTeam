# Research Report

Research ID: `RES-20260828T090235Z-9CB179A1`

The reviewed current samples do not support “the community agrees.” Complaints cluster around particular dungeons and pulls: enrage pressure, dangerous trash, mechanic density, forced DPS downtime, pacing, and uneven tuning. In the same window, other players say +10s feel comparable to last season, report enjoying the season, or argue that reaching +13 in week one means progression is too easy.

The disagreement is partly segmented by key level, role, player skill and group type. Blizzard's stated design goal was Season 1-like overall difficulty with more responsibility on DPS, slightly higher creature health and stronger target-priority checks. Launch-week dungeon adjustments confirm active tuning, not which opinion is prevalent.

Coverage: two r/wow threads and one Blizzard General Discussion thread from 2026-08-19 through 2026-08-28. Reddit and Blizzard Forums remain separate venues. This is a qualitative theme map, not a survey or prevalence estimate.
