Question 31 recorded both agreement and disagreement that adults should complete more age checks for child safety; the consultation is not treated here as a representative population poll.
Findings cite support for reliable child-safety enforcement and concerns about circumvention, privacy, overseas data transfers, government overreach, exclusion, displacement, cost, and business burden. Respondents called for robust, privacy-preserving, accessible, risk-based systems, oversight, education, alternatives, and platform accountability.
