# Research Report

Research ID: `RES-20260828T073255Z-46A8F500`

Current public arguments divide into three clusters. Supporters emphasize child protection and enforceable age boundaries. Opponents emphasize privacy, surveillance, censorship, breach risk, exclusion, circumvention, and displacement to less regulated services. A conditional middle position accepts age checks only when they disclose the minimum age attribute, offer alternatives and appeals, undergo independent scrutiny, and do not silently become identity tracking.

The venues differ. The sampled r/privacy thread was strongly hostile within that privacy-focused sample. The sampled Hacker News discussion was contested and technically focused on whether anonymous credentials can resist copying or enforcement pressure. The UK consultation recorded both support and substantial privacy/effectiveness concerns. None of these venue observations is a population-prevalence claim.
