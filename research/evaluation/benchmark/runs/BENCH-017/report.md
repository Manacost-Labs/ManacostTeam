# Research Report

Research ID: `RES-20260828T091313Z-D099FD24`

For recurring professional editing, choose the 14-inch MacBook Pro with at least M5 Pro unless the actual workload is mostly short, light or proxy-based. The Pro costs more, but adds active cooling, higher-tier M5 Pro/Max options, broader port/display support and more memory/storage headroom.

The M5 Air remains an excellent mobile editor for short 4K jobs, proxies and occasional exports. An independent Handbrake test finished in 4:41, but a sustained raw-video Premiere comparison placed the Air at 52% of the Pro's score. That gap is more relevant to long professional exports than burst benchmarks.

Polish Apple list pricing observed: MacBook Air from 6,499 PLN; 14-inch MacBook Pro from 8,499 PLN, with M5 Pro configurations higher. Final configuration depends on codec, effects, timeline length, external displays, ports, memory and budget.
