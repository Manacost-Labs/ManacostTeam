# Research Report

Research ID: `RES-20260828T090235Z-0982E12B`

There is no defensible universal winner. In the inspected all-regions top-end sample, Arms Warrior and Arcane Mage were joint leaders at about 28% of DPS slots, followed by Elemental Shaman at about 24.8%. The sample contains 960 timed runs and includes runs within three key levels of each dungeon's highest timed key, which prevents easier dungeons from fully dominating the pool.

That is representation in successful groups, not proof of causal damage superiority. The answer changes by dungeon, key band, region, composition and whether the metric is raw damage, survival, utility or group success. Expert rankings also include utility and survivability. Blizzard's scheduled tuning and August 26 hotfix window make this a dated snapshot, not a season-long verdict.
