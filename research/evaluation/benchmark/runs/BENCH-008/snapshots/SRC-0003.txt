The Go project runs both end-to-end and microbenchmark suites.
Its benchmark set is curated and may change because a fixed set can optimize the wrong thing.
Performance numbers are not reported in isolation: every experiment is compared with a baseline.
Measurements separated in time can contain substantial machine or VM noise.
