Go 1.20+ supports profile-guided optimization. As of Go 1.22, a representative program set showed around 2-14% performance improvement with PGO.
Profiles should represent actual production behavior. Unrepresentative profiles may yield little or no production improvement.
Constructing and maintaining representative benchmarks is difficult. Microbenchmarks are usually bad PGO candidates because they exercise only a small part of the application.
