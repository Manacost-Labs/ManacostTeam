# Research Report

Research ID: `RES-20260828T091313Z-9C48E7D8`

If “real-world” means very light Wi-Fi browsing/reading with a low-refresh display, the strongest inspected result is the 2026 Dell XPS 14 at roughly 43 hours. Dell documents that its panel can fall to 1 Hz under static content, which helps this exact workload.

That is not a universal winner. Another review measured only 12 hours 19 minutes for the XPS 14 under moderately intensive browsing and streaming. In a separate consistent 150-nit continuous-web dataset, the M5 MacBook Pro reached about 18 hours. These values cannot be merged into an exact ranking because workload, brightness, panel, refresh policy, OS and background activity differ.

Choose a workload first: light browsing, mixed office/video, coding, or heavy CPU/GPU. Then rank only models tested in the same lab, method and configuration, and verify that exact configuration is currently purchasable.
